# Quickref — Modular Cognitive Aperture

**Law:** orient → grant → evidence → receipt
**Boundary:** disclosure only; ingestion and canonical Shape formation are dependencies
**Current stage:** A — readiness and contracts

## Always

- admit before ranking;
- deny before include;
- resolve evidence lazily;
- budget whole blocks;
- preserve branch, scope, maturity, and provenance;
- separate execution from audit;
- abstain when dependencies are stale or unavailable.

## Never

- confidence-only admission;
- forced fallback evidence;
- suppressed content in execution;
- full-ocean scans on request paths;
- duplicate source or Shape stores;
- Shape promotion inside disclosure;
- incognito retrieval or durable learning;
- surface-specific policy reinterpretation.
