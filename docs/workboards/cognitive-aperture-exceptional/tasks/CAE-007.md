# CAE-007 — Receipts and observability

**Stage:** C
**Priority:** high
**Depends on:** CAE-005A
**Owner paths:** receipt owner, Bridge/Holodeck inspect paths

## Outcome

Every disclosed, empty, denied, or abstained result is reconstructible from a compact privacy-aware receipt.

## Acceptance

- receipt records corpus/policy revisions, effective grant, decisions, block IDs, budget ledger, and result status;
- sensitive evidence text is referenced rather than duplicated;
- incognito stores hashes/metrics only;
- retention and deletion behavior tested;
- one full result reconstruction demonstrated.
