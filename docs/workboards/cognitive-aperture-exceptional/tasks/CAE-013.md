# CAE-013 — Corpus readiness contract and fixture corpus

**Stage:** A
**Priority:** critical
**Depends on:** CAE-000
**Owner paths:** library/runtime status owner, fixture data, readiness tests

## Outcome

The aperture can distinguish ready, empty-valid, stale, interrupted, and unsupported corpus states before retrieval.

## Acceptance

- `CorpusCatalog` contract reports revision, counts, coverage, capabilities, and stale state;
- fixture corpus covers positive, negative, distractor, privacy, multi-dimensional Shape, and AntiMatch cases;
- raw source stored once with fragments and provenance;
- readiness failure produces abstention;
- no production corpus content committed as a test fixture.
