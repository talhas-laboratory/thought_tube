# Reusable Templates

## Template 1: Niche Q&A Post

Best for:

- product
- career
- creator advice
- operator newsletters

Structure:

1. `Headline`
2. `Subtitle`
3. Short issue-brand line
4. Reader question in blockquote
5. Why this question matters now
6. One early proof artifact
7. Your thesis
8. 3 to 5 section body
9. Paywall after the thesis and first useful proof
10. Practical close

Example use:

- `How AI will impact product management`

## Template 2: Technical Explainer

Best for:

- engineering
- science
- market mechanics
- systems thinking

Structure:

1. `Headline` with direct search intent
2. Why the concept matters in the real world
3. The core problem
4. Bullet list of ambiguity / tradeoffs
5. Scope statement: what this article covers
6. Section 1
7. Section 2
8. Section 3
9. Paywall after orientation and one meaningful section
10. Closing recap or next piece

Example use:

- `The Must-Know Fundamentals of Distributed Systems`

## Template 3: Data-Led Reported Brief

Best for:

- startup/VC
- finance
- policy
- industry intelligence

Structure:

1. `Headline` with claim
2. `Subtitle` naming the dataset
3. What people thought would happen
4. Short reversal
5. Key stat
6. Chart
7. One interpretive section heading
8. Paywall
9. Deep analysis below the lock

Example use:

- `No Mere Reversion to the Mean`

## Template 4: Narrative Thesis Essay

Best for:

- strategy
- markets
- culture/tech crossover
- founder essays

Structure:

1. `Headline` naming the big idea
2. `Subtitle` with a few concrete hooks
3. Sponsor block separated by divider
4. Conversational hello
5. Real anecdote
6. Extract the principle
7. Run 2 to 4 additional examples
8. State the tradeoff
9. Concluding aphorism or synthesis line
10. Signature / CTA

Example use:

- `A Market For (Almost) Everything`

## Template 5: Issue-Based Newsletter

Best for:

- publications with recurring columns
- multi-topic operator newsletters
- weekly "edition" products

Structure:

1. `Headline` for the anchor essay
2. Secondary line listing side topics
3. `Contents`
4. Brief house note
5. Anchor essay
6. Secondary items
7. Jobs / links / events / recommendations
8. Share / subscribe CTA

Example use:

- `Your Life is (Almost) a Call Option`

## Recommended Default Structure

If you only adopt one structure, use this:

1. `Headline`: specific promise
2. `Subtitle`: who it helps and what angle it takes
3. `Opening`: anecdote, question, or stat
4. `Thesis`: paragraph 2 to 4
5. `Roadmap`: what the piece covers
6. `Body`: 3 to 5 section headings
7. `Evidence`: examples, charts, screenshots, quotes
8. `Paywall`: after the reader has already learned something useful
9. `Close`: implication + next move

## What the Best Niche Posts Rarely Do

- generic inspirational lead-ins
- unclear headlines
- long autobiographical setup before the topic
- giant unbroken text walls
- hiding the actual thesis until late
- paywalling before any real value appears
- ending without synthesis
